# ============================================================================
#  FURIOUS FOWLS — sounds.  Edit freely, then run make.
#
#  The CPC's AY-3-8912 has three channels. This engine gives each one a list
#  of STEPS, one step at a time, one frame at a time. A step says where the
#  pitch starts and which way it is sliding, where the volume starts and
#  which way it is fading, and whether the noise generator is mixed in. That
#  is enough for every cartoon noise there is: a slide is a swoop, a fast
#  fade is a blip, and noise is anything that breaks.
#
#      step <frames> <period> <slide> <vol> <vfade> <noise>
#
#  frames  how long this step lasts, 1..255
#  period  PSG tone period. Pitch = 125000 / period, so LOWER IS HIGHER.
#          120 is about a top C, 500 a low one. 0 means silent.
#  slide   added to the period every frame, -128..127. Positive slides DOWN
#          in pitch (the period is getting longer). This is the swoop.
#  vol     0..15
#  vfade   added to the volume every frame, -8..7. Negative is a decay.
#  noise   0 = pure tone. 1..31 mixes the noise generator in at that period
#          (1 is a hiss, 31 a rumble). Add 32 to drop the tone and leave
#          only noise — that is what a crash is.
#
#  Channel A is the bird and the menus, B is the fort coming apart, C is the
#  pigs. A new sound on a channel simply replaces whatever was there, so a
#  second collapse interrupts the first rather than queueing behind it.
# ============================================================================

# ---- channel A: the bird ---------------------------------------------------

sound stretch           # ONE notch of the pull: a short creak of rubber.
  step  2  700 -110   9   0   5    # It fires again at every notch, and there
  step  3  480  -70   7  -3   7    # are eight of them, so keep it under the
                                   # gap between notches or you only ever
                                   # hear this first step.

sound launch            # ...and the twang when it lets go. A real boing is
  step  2  110   -8  14   0   0    # a pitch that snaps UP and wobbles down
  step  3   70   30  14   0   0
  step  3  160  -34  13  -1   0
  step  4   58   44  11  -2   0
  step  5  240   30   7  -2   0

sound miss              # nothing but dirt, or gone over the horizon: the
  step  4  300   30  11   0   0    # small descending noise of a wasted bird
  step  4  420   45   9  -1   0
  step  6  600   60   7  -1   0
